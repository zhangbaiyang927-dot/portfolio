Hi, I'm Maddy Zhang.

A marketer driven by curiosity and grounded in practice. I believe marketing is never only about tactics or strategy — at its core, it's a way of understanding people and building real connection.

I stay open to new ideas, through writing, filmmaking, and project work, through conferences and exhibitions, meeting people from different fields and letting their perspectives sharpen my own.

Outside of work, I'm a devoted lover of music, travel and photography, always chasing the small moments that spark creativity and beauty. I believe the best ideas are born from observation and conversation.
