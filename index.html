WORK 1：从0到1 — 一场针对"价格敏感型新手"的IMC精准战役

整合营销 新品上市 全链路闭环 B端渠道激活
01
Why did we do this?

"The market isn't short on good products — it's short on reasons for users to dare and try."

Russia's nicotine-alternative market is growing fast, but the mental landscape around it is crowded and confusing. Mainstream brands cluster around a "high-performance" position, fighting over existing users with complex specs and premium pricing — while overlooking the largest pool of untapped growth.

Price-sensitive first-time users want to try new-format products, but two barriers stand in the way:

  • Cognitive barrier — specs are confusing, categories are unclear, "what's right for me" is a mystery
  • Price barrier — unwilling to pay a premium for something they're not sure they'll like
Conclusion

Our brand equity concentrates on technical innovation — but lacks approachability in mainstream perception. This product's mission was never to be "a cheap product." Its job is to become the brand's first touchpoint with new users.

Why This Order?
  • Price is the hook — without a low entry price, the target user won't even notice the brand
  • Experience is the converter — only after touching and trying does "low price ≠ low quality" become believable
  • Relationship is the moat — competitors can copy a price, but they can't copy an already-established user habit

Offline: Precision B2B2C Penetration

"Not asking B2B to sell for the brand — helping B2B serve their own customers better."

What the Maverick & OXVA Tasting Event Taught Us

Maverick had retail footprint but lacked brand recognition; OXVA had a mature product line but limited regional penetration — a straightforward exchange of strengths.

Why Novosibirsk?
  • Not a first-tier city (Moscow / St. Petersburg), so competitive pressure is lower
  • Regional users are more open to new brands, giving early movers an advantage
  • Maverick already had a solid retail network in the city
